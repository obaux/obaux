**Title:** Your account: a new number, a copy, or leaving Pam

**Who it's for:** Everyone

**Summary:** What to do when you get a new phone number, want a copy of what Pam keeps, or want to delete your
account.

**Lead:** You sign in with your phone number. There is no password to lose. (`terms.s.your-account.p1`)

## You got a new phone number
- Call Pam and tell us, so you can still sign in. (`terms.s.your-account.p3`)
- Keep your account to yourself. Anyone holding your phone can get in. (`terms.s.your-account.p2`)

## You want a copy of what Pam keeps
- Call Pam and ask for a copy of what we keep about you. We will check that it is really you first. (`privacy.copy.body`)

## You want to delete your account
- Call Pam and ask us to delete your account. We will check that it is really you, then delete it. (`privacy.delete.body`)
- More about what Pam keeps, and for how long: the Privacy page.
