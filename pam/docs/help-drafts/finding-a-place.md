**Title:** Finding a place

**Who it's for:** Residents

**Summary:** How to find a place that can help near you, see what it offers, and keep it for later.

**Lead:** Pam lists places in your city that help with school, work, home and family.

## Look by kind of help
- Open Explore. (`tab.explore`)
- Pick a kind of place: School and training, Work and money, or Home and family. (`explore.categories`, `category.*`)
- Each has smaller groups, like Food, Job openings, GED or high school, or A place to live. (`category.sub.*`)

## Search by name or address
- Type in the search box at the top: "Search programs". (`explore.search.placeholder`)
- Nothing found? Check the spelling, or try a shorter word. (`places.search.none.body`)

## Look near you
- Places show nearest first. To look somewhere else, change the area with a ZIP code or an address. (`places.areaLabel`)
- Pam does not save where you look. (`places.areaPrivacy`)

## Open a place
- You see what it does, its address, and how to get there.
- Opening hours may be samples while Pam checks the real ones, so call before you go. (`places.source`)

## Keep it for later
- Tap "Save this place". It goes to Saved, ready when you need the address. (`place.save`, `saved.empty.body`)
- Ready to go? See Planning a visit.
