**Title:** Your safety

**Who it's for:** Everyone

**Summary:** What to do if you are in danger, in crisis, or a message feels unsafe.

## In danger right now?
**Call 911.** (`help.safety.danger.*`, `terms.s.emergencies.p1`)

Pam is not an emergency service. Nobody at Pam watches messages as they come in.

## In crisis?
**Call or text 988.** Someone answers, day and night. (`terms.s.emergencies.p2`)

## Tell Pam what happened
- Write to us now: [the "Still stuck?" box or hello@joinpam.org, shown here at the top, not at the end].
- Tell us what happened. You don't have to explain everything. (`help.safety.call.body`)
- A person at Pam reads every message.

## A message feels unsafe
- In the chat, tap ⋯ then "Report suspicious activity". (`help.safety.message.body`)
- When you report, Pam sees only the last message they sent you. (`help.safety.message.note`)
- You can also block the person. More: Messages in Pam.
