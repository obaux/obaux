**Title:** Talking to someone at Pam

**Who it's for:** Everyone

**Summary:** How to call a real person at Pam, when we answer, and what to do when we are closed.

**Lead:** Someone at Pam can help you. You do not need to know what to ask for. (`help.intro`)

## Call us
- Open Pam and tap "Need help? Call Pam". (`help.needHelp`)
- We answer Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. The call is free. (`help.call.body`)

## If we are closed
- Leave a message and we will call you back the next day we are open. (`help.closed.body`)

## What we can help with (`help.what.*`)
- Getting into the app
- Finding a place that can help
- Changing or missing a visit
- Reaching your guide
- Anything else. If we cannot help, we will find someone who can.

## In danger right now?
- Call 911 first. You don't need to call Pam. (`help.safety.danger.body`)

## A message feels unsafe
- In the chat, tap ⋯ then "Report suspicious activity". (`help.safety.message.body`)
- When you report, Pam sees only the last message they sent you. (`help.safety.message.note`)
- More: Messages in Pam.
