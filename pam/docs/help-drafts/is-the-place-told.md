**Title:** Is the place told about my visit?

**Who it's for:** Residents and providers

**Summary:** Some places see your planned visit in Pam, and some don't. Here is how to tell which, and what to do.

**Lead:** Pam lists two kinds of places. Some are run by a program lead on Pam. Others come from public lists, like
the city's own pages.

## A place with a program lead on Pam
- The program sees your visit in Pam, on its calendar, and can check you in when you arrive. (`join.privacy.provider.1`)
- You can message the program from its page.

## A place from a public list
- Pam does not tell the place you are coming. Your visit is only there to help you plan. (`place.noLead`)
- When you plan the visit, Pam says so: "{place} is not on Pam as a program, so it is not told about this visit."
  (`trips.booked.noLead`)
- If you need an appointment, call the place. Its phone number is on its page.

## For providers: why you weren't told
- You see visits on your calendar only for a program you added to Pam. (D-498)
- If your place is on Pam but you didn't add it, add your program. More: Keeping your program's listing up to date.

## Good to know
- Pam doesn't send the place a text or an email about your visit, either way.
