# a86 — the bell hides while search is open (Will, 17 October)

`bell-390.png` and `bell-320.png`: left column at rest (bell with its dot), right column tapped (no bell, the bar wider, Cancel on its right). Rows: English light, English dark, Arabic light.

A member whose Messages tab is hidden (nobody to message) has the bell on Explore. While the search bar is open the bell is not drawn and not in the accessibility tree; Cancel or Escape bring it back with its dot, and focus goes to the launcher (it did not before: Cancel removed the focused button and focus fell to the page).
