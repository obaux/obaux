# A new phone number can move your account

- Got a new phone number? "New number?" on Sign in moves your account to it. Pam texts a code to the new number, you
  tell us about the account you had, and a person at Pam checks it is really you before anything moves.
- The super admin sees the request on Requests, with what was typed beside what the account says, and ticks "I spoke to
  this person" before approving. If the answer is no, the new number's sign-in is deleted.
