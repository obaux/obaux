# When a save on the Pam team's screens fails, the small alert says so

The Pam team's own saves follow the rule Text alerts already uses. A Demo view switch in Everyone moves only once its save
succeeds; while it saves it shows that it's working. When it fails, or there's no answer in 15 seconds, it stays where it was.
Saving Pam's hours, closing or reopening a conversation with Pam, making an invite and deciding on a staff request work the
same way when Pam fails: instead of a line or a card on the page, the one small alert at the bottom says "That didn't go
through. It's not your fault, so try again." Each failure goes to the error log, with only its action and a code. A reason
the person can act on (pick a day, another city, already decided) still shows next to what it is about. (D-616)
