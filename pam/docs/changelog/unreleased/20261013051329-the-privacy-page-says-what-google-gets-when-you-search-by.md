# The Privacy page says what Google gets when you search by address

When street-address search is switched on, Pam's server (not your phone) sends the words you typed to Google to suggest places, and asks Google where the one you pick is. The Privacy page now says what Google gets (those words, the place you pick and a code made new for each search), what it does not (your name, your phone number, your phone's IP address), and that Pam keeps neither what you type nor what Google answers (D-552). The search stays off until the Privacy page says it, in every language.
