# The reminder text arrives the day before your visit, even for an evening visit

A visit at 9 pm or later used to get its reminder on the morning of the visit, still saying "tomorrow". It now arrives on the day before, outside your quiet hours (for example 8:55 pm for a 9:30 pm visit). If you turn texts on after planning visits, those visits get their reminder too. A long place name is no longer cut in the middle of a word.
