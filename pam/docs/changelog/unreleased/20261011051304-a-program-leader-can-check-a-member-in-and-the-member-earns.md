# A program leader can mark a person as arrived, and the person earns points

When someone comes to a program on Pam, the program's leader taps the circle beside their visit. It is saved, the person earns 100 points once for that visit, and the leader can undo a mistaken tap for 14 days (the points are taken back once, never twice). A leader can only do this for people who planned a visit at their own program. (D-526)
