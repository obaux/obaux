# Policy pages: Back returns to Sign in, tabs and Back to top behave; no flash of the wrong screen while loading or on a super admin's Profile

On the privacy notice and the terms, Back now returns to Sign in even after you have used the section tabs, a tapped tab puts its heading just under the top bars, and "Back to top" goes all the way to the top. While Pam checks who is signed in it now shows the new layout's top bar instead of the old page, and a super admin's Profile no longer flashes a member's profile first. (D-492)
