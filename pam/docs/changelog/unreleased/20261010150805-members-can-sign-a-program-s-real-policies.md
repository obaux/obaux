# You can sign a program's policies in Pam

When a program has put its policies in Pam, its page lists them, you read each page, and you sign with your finger or by typing your name. Before you sign, Pam says what the program keeps: a record of the policies you sign, with the date. Your signature picture stays private to you. If a program replaces a policy with a new version, you are asked to sign the new one, and what you signed before stays on record. Signing never stops you booking a visit.
