# A case manager can limit, pause or hand over a member; the Pam team chooses each member's guide

A case manager now has two rows on a member's page. **Limit or pause** sets what
the member can do (everything, limited, or paused) and asks for a reason, which
only staff can read. **Hand over** gives the member to another case manager in
the same city. On Everyone, the Pam team sees each member's guide, can list the
members who have none, and can choose a guide for anyone. Until now nothing in
the app could limit anyone or assign a guide, so the limited-account notice never
appeared, and a member who joined without a case manager was seen by no one.
(D-446)
