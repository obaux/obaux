# The two texts that used to give a phone number now reach people in their own language

When a staff request is not approved, and when parts of Pam are turned off for someone, the text no longer gives a
phone number. It points to joinpam.org/support. It now says so in Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional),
Russian and Arabic, each in one message, and no longer falls back to English. In Spanish, "Need help? Contact Pam" now speaks
to the member as "tú", like the rest of the Help screen (D-514).
