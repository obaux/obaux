# No made-up hours on the real app

Pam no longer shows opening hours it does not have. A place with no real hours has no "Open until…" or "Closed" line, no hours row and no "sample hours" note,
in Explore, saved places, Connect and on the place page; instead a "Check hours on Google" row takes you to the place on Google Maps. The six places with
real hours are unchanged (D-624).
