# Sign in: Privacy and Terms no longer cover the code button on short phones; small buttons are 48px

On a short phone (an iPhone SE, or an Android with the browser bar showing) the Privacy, Terms and About links no longer sit over the "Send me a code" button, so tapping the button always sends the code. They come after the content instead, at the bottom of the screen when there is room. The Trips banner's "Sign", the policy screen's "Done" and the signature sheet's "Clear" are now big enough to tap (48px). (D-500)
