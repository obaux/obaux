# The Support pages explain how Pam works, with steps, tables and screenshots

Pam's website now has ten help articles for the parts of Pam people get stuck on most: who your
guide is, which texts Pam sends and why one might not come, who can see what about you (a table),
how to join with a link or a code, how case managers and program leads join, what Pam's special
words mean, using Pam as both a member and a program, sending an invite, messages, and points
and badges. Each one says who it is for, gives numbered steps in the words on the screen, shows
the screen, and ends with how to reach a person at Pam. They say only what Pam does today.
