# The Pam website has a new home page

The home page on joinpam.org has a new look: a new header and footer, a picture of Philadelphia, and three
rows that show Pam on a phone for residents, programs and case managers. The pictures at the top are cards you
can tap to flip through; they drift gently and flip by themselves until you press Pause. Everything holds still if
your device asks for less motion (D-529).
