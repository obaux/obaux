# Text alerts say what is not sent yet, and a STOP stays a STOP

The Text alerts screen no longer offers switches for texts Pam does not send yet: each says
"Coming soon", and the one that is sent (a saved place closing or moving) is saved on the
account, not on one phone, with "Saved." when it is. Text reminders says plainly what is sent
today and drops the promise of "a few messages a week". Somebody who replied STOP is told texts
are off and is not asked again; the database now refuses to let the app clear a STOP. The
waiting-list answer says what really happens. Four alert texts are drafted, unsigned and
unsent (D-453).
