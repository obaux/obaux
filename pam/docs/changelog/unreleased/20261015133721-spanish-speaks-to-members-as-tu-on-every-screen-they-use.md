# Spanish speaks to members as tú on every screen they use

In Spanish, the screens a member reads, and the ones members and staff both use (sign-in, Messages, text reminders, alerts, notices, Help, Your points, What your guide can see), now all say "tú". Before, "Su visita está reservada" sat above "Léelas antes de tu visita" on the same screen. Screens only staff see (the case manager's Home, Schedule, the program screens) are "usted" throughout, as are Privacy and Terms (D-577).
