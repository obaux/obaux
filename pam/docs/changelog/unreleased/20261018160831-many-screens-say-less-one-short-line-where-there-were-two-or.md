# Many screens say less: one short line where there were two or three

About thirty screens now say one short line where they said two or three, in all seven languages: empty lists, the blocked-conversation
confirmation, the program review steps, invite links, who a recommendation goes to, and the like. Lines that promised something Pam does
not do are gone, and what Pam does or never does is explained on the Privacy and Terms pages instead (Will, 18 October). (D-608)
