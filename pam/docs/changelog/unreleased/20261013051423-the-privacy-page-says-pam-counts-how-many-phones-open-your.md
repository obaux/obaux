# The Privacy page says Pam counts how many phones open Your points and Profile

Each day Pam now counts how many phones opened Your points and Profile: one number for each screen, for everyone together, with no name, phone number or account in it. To count a phone once a day, the phone keeps a note that stays on it. In reports, a number under 5 shows only as "under 5". The Privacy page says so under "What we keep", in every language (D-555).
