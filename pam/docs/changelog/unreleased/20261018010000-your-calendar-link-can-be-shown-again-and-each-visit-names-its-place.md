# Your calendar link can be shown again, and each visit names its place

The Calendar screen now asks which calendar you use, says when you set it up, and always has
buttons to add or copy your link, a new link, or disconnect. Each visit in your calendar shows
the place's name and address and its time. From a booked trip you can add that one visit to your
calendar from your phone. Pam does not see which calendar you pick (D-604).
