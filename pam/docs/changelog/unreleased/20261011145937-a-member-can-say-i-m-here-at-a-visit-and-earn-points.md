# You can say "I'm here" at a visit and earn points

On the page of a visit that is on now, tap I'm here. Pam asks where your phone is once, checks that you are at the place, and gives you 100 points. It does not keep where you are, only that you were there. If you would rather not share it, tap I went: 60 points, and up to three a week. It works at any place, whether or not a program leader runs it on Pam. (D-535)
