# The privacy page says who gets email addresses, when a member is asked for one, and what is kept when you sign

In all seven languages: a company that sends email for us gets the address and the email whenever Pam sends one, and may not use them for anything else; a member is
asked for an email only if their invite link has run out, and it is deleted once the new link is sent (it used to say members are never asked); and when a program asks you to
sign its rules, Pam keeps what you signed, the date and your signature, only you see the signature, and the program sees your first name and the date. (D-490)
