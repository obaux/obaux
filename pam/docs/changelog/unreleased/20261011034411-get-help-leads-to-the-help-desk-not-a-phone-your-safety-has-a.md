# Get help leads to the help desk, not a phone; Your safety has a one-tap 988 row

Pam has no phone line, so the app no longer asks you to call it. On Get help, the first row is now "Contact Pam" and
opens Pam's help desk on the website, where answers come first and writing to Pam is the next step. Your safety keeps
"Call 911" and adds a row, "Call or text 988", that opens your phone's dialler in one tap, next to "Talk to someone now",
which opens the help desk. The error and notice messages that said "call Pam" now say "contact Pam", the privacy page's
contact line gives Pam's email address, and a wrong sign-in code says to check it or tap Send again. Pam also
no longer says it will text you when a place you saved closes, because texts are not on yet (D-514, D-515).
