# A case manager's Home shows each member's visits, not points

Each member on a case manager's Home now shows one line about their visits: the next one, or whether their most recent
visit was marked as there, and a chip when it was not. Points are on the member's page. "Invite someone" and "What you
can see" are two rows under the list, and "What you can see" opens the full list of what a guide sees. (Card a93, D-596.)
