# Privacy and Terms: the highlighted tab follows what you are reading, and the tabs slide to keep it in view

On the Privacy notice and the Terms, the highlighted tab is now the section you have scrolled to the top, so it no longer gets confused when two sections are on screen, and a tab you tap stays highlighted while the page slides there. The row of tabs slides to keep the highlighted one fully in view, and after a tap, screen readers and keyboards move to that section's heading. (D-497)
