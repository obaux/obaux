The page now says that Pam keeps a last name (if you give one) and a city, and that only the Pam team can see them; what it
keeps if Pam is not in your city yet; what a report about a place keeps and who reads it; that a case manager or a program
lead can add a photo of themselves, who can see it, and that the old one is deleted when it is changed or the account goes;
and that the services a program lists are shown with the program. All seven languages. "Last updated" is 17 October 2026.
