# Pam's hours: each day is a full row you can tap anywhere

On Pam's hours, each day is now a full-width row, 49px tall with a line under it, and a tap anywhere on the row ticks it.
Before, the days were small boxes 32px apart whose tap areas overlapped, so a tap near the line between two days could tick
the wrong one (Iris C1). (D-605)
