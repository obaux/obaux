# You can add your Pam visits to your calendar

On Profile, choose Calendar and make a link. Paste it into Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook, and your Pam visits show up there, moved or removed when the visit is. By default each one says only "Pam visit" and its time; one switch adds the place's name and address. The link is a secret address: anyone who has it can read your visits, so make a new link any time and the old one stops. (D-538)
