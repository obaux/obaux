# A case manager can ask to join from the website

- "Join as a case manager" on the website opens a page in the app. You sign in with your phone first, then give
  your name, the city you work in, where you work and your work email. A person on the Pam team checks every
  request by hand and texts you the answer; you can also sign in to see it.
- If the answer is no, you can ask again. If you already have a Pam account, the page tells you to contact Pam.
- The super admin sees where a case manager works and their work email on the request. On a yes, the email is kept
  with their account, where only the Pam team can see it.
- A request that was refused is deleted 30 days after the answer, and one nobody answered 90 days after it was made,
  with the sign-in that made it if that person has no account.
