# You can connect Google Calendar and your visits stay in it

On the Calendar page, connect your Google account and Pam makes a calendar called "Pam visits" and keeps it up to date: a visit you plan, move or cancel in Pam changes there within 15 minutes. Pam can see and change only that calendar, not your others. Disconnect deletes it. By default each event says only "Pam visit" and its time; one switch adds the place's name and address. (D-539)
