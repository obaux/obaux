# A visit nobody marked as arrived is shown as missed to the program, and costs you nothing

At a program that runs on Pam, a visit that nobody marked as arrived is marked missed a day after it ends. It does not take points away, and nobody is texted. The program's leader can still mark the person as arrived for 14 days, and then the person earns the points as usual. Places that no leader runs on Pam are not marked at all. (D-526)
