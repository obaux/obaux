# Support: Does the place know I'm coming? now says which places see your visit

On the Support site, "Does the place know I'm coming?" no longer says Pam tells no place about your visit. It now says that programs on Pam see the visits planned with them (first name, day, time, service, and whether you were marked as there), that other places are not told, and how to mark your own visit with "I'm here" or "I went". (D-531)
