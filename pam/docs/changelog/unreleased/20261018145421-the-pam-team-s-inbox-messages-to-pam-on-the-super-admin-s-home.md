# The Pam team's inbox: Messages to Pam on the super admin's Home

The super admin's Home now begins with "Messages to Pam" and the number of people waiting for a reply. It opens the Pam
team's inbox:
- Who is waiting comes first, longest wait first, and each says how long they have waited.
- Conversations already answered come next.
- Closed conversations are one row at the foot.
- Each shows a first name and a role (with the area, or the program a lead runs) and the first line of the last message.
- A line says how quickly half of Pam's first replies came over the last 30 days.

It stays hidden until Message Pam is switched on. (Card a100, D-605.)
