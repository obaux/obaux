# Support search forgives typos, knows everyday words and suggests as you type

On the website's Support page, the search box now suggests likely questions as you type (with the article that answers each), understands a typing mistake ("invte" finds Sending an invite), and knows everyday words for the same thing ("sms" finds Texts from Pam; "caseworker" finds Who is my guide?). When nothing matches it offers "Did you mean…", and the front page lists the questions people ask most (D-507).
