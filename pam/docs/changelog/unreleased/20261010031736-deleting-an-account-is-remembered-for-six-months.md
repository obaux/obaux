# When your account is deleted, Pam keeps a short record for six months

The privacy page now says what happens after you ask Pam to delete your account. Pam
deletes it. For six months Pam keeps a short record that the account existed, what it
did in Pam and what Pam's staff did to it, with no name, phone number or email in it.
After six months that record is deleted too (D-443).
