Iris. The merge desk's note at 02:09: re-test b64 (`c67be570`) on a build made for tests.

- **Press part 2:** every card and tab I found unpressed in P1, held with forced `:active`, light
  and dark, at 320 and 390.
- **The schedule:** the example schedule seen on a Sunday; the coming week should not be empty.

## How

- **Build:** Storybook from `main` `c67be570`, served on 127.0.0.1. It names only the stub project.
  Every other host was refused, and Chromium's updater and speech feature were off.
- **The setup:** Playwright in Chromium with touch, at 390×844 and 320×568.
- **Light and dark:** set with Storybook's own Light / Dark switch (`globals=theme:dark`). Pam's page
  follows that switch, not the browser's setting. My first dark pass used the browser setting and
  drew light, so it was thrown away and run again.
- **Press:** I forced `:active` on the control and every element around it, as a finger does. I then
  read the shade (the background and its colour) and the scale (the transform). I also ran it once
  with reduced motion on, at 390.
- **The schedule:** seen on this machine's real clock (about 02:15 UTC on Sunday 18 October, which is
  Saturday evening in New York). Then I pinned the page's clock in New York time to Sunday
  18 October, Monday 19, Wednesday 21, Saturday 24, Sunday 25 and Sunday 1 November. For each,
  I read Home (week and day, then the next two weeks) and Your calendar.
