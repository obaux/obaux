### W4: speaking a message that can't start. **PASS**

I tapped the microphone in a conversation with the recogniser failing each way the browser can
report:

| What the browser reports | What the conversation says (en) |
|---|---|
| `not-allowed`, `service-not-allowed` | **Pam can't use the microphone.** Allow the microphone for Pam in your browser's settings, then tap the microphone again. You can type your message instead. |
| `audio-capture` | **Pam can't find a microphone.** Check that one is plugged in, then tap the microphone again. You can type your message instead. |
| `network` | **Speaking needs an internet connection.** Check your connection and tap the microphone again. You can type your message instead. |
| `language-not-supported` | **This browser can't listen in your language.** You can type your message instead. |
| an unexpected code, a recogniser that throws, or one that ends without starting | **The microphone didn't start.** Tap the microphone again, or type your message instead. |

- **Arabic:** each line is in Arabic and the banner reads right to left. Example: "التحدث يحتاج
  إلى اتصال بالإنترنت", `shots/2026-10-18-rt65-dictation-offline-ar-320.png`.
- **Fit:** at 390 and 320 the banner sits 24px from each edge and nothing in it is cut
  (`shots/2026-10-18-rt65-dictation-blocked-en-320.png`). It has a Close ("Close this message",
  "إغلاق هذه الرسالة"). "Listening" never shows.
- **What isn't a failure stays quiet.** A recogniser that starts, then hears nothing (`no-speech`)
  or is stopped (`aborted`), shows no banner. Network failing after it started shows the connection
  line.

  A note on my own test: when I first fired `no-speech` and `aborted` *without* a start, the app
  said "The microphone didn't start". That's right, because nothing started; a real browser always
  starts first.
- **No recogniser at all:** in a browser without speech recognition, the microphone isn't shown.
