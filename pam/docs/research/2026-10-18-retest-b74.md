# Re-test on main at b74: Text alerts (D-615) and P3 (18 October 2026)

Iris. The merge desk's note at 19:58 said b74 is on main at `e1a2cbec` and asked me to re-test two
things there:

- Text alerts as Will drew it (D-615);
- P3, where the after-hours reply keeps the hours that were in force when it was sent. P3 is behind
  the switch, so I tested it where my harness turns the switch on.

"Something is wrong here" is unchanged, as Will asked, so it isn't covered here.

## How

- **App:** the `build:e2e` app of `e1a2cbec` on 127.0.0.1. Every other host was refused, and
  Chromium's updater and speech feature were off.
- **Data:** made-up people, with the database's answers given by my test the way `alerts.spec.ts` and
  `message-pam.spec.ts` give them. The phone number on the stub account was a 555 number.
- **What was never touched:** no account was made, nothing was sent, and nothing reached the live
  project. The switch (`pam_messaging_live`) and `texts_live` were on only in my stub.
- **The setup:** Playwright in Chromium with touch, at 320×568.
- **Who and how:** three kinds of account (a member, a program lead, a case manager), in English,
  Arabic, Russian and Spanish, light and dark.

## The result

**Text alerts matches Will's rules (Foundations › Patterns, D-615) for all three accounts, in every
language I tried:**

- no card;
- one row per kind, divided by thin lines;
- the badge under the name;
- what is on first, with "Coming soon" below it;
- no STOP line and no Privacy link;
- nothing cut;
- no sideways scroll;
- in Arabic, it mirrors.

The agree screen is now the only way to say yes:

- a switch with no yes on file records nothing and opens the agree screen;
- "Agree" brings the person back with that switch on, and "Not now" with it off;
- after a STOP every switch is off and can't be turned on.

**P3 passes.** Each after-hours reply shows the hours that were in force when it was sent. A reply from
before the change shows today's hours, as intended.

There are four small findings: on the agree screen, on a write that fails, and on the size of what turns a setting on. There is one
privacy note for the desk, about a phone number in the test files.

## P3: the after-hours reply keeps its hours

Today's hours in my stub were Monday to Friday, 9 to 5, New York time. Three old replies were in one
thread:

| The reply carried | It reads (en) | It reads (ar) |
|---|---|---|
| Mon–Sat, 08:00–16:30, New York | "Pam is here Monday to Saturday, 8 AM to 4:30 PM Eastern time" | "تتواجد Pam من الاثنين إلى السبت، من 8 ص إلى 4:30 م (التوقيت الشرقي)" |
| No hours (sent before `20261018182004`) | "Pam is here Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM Eastern time" (today's) | "…من الاثنين إلى الجمعة، من 9 ص إلى 5 م (التوقيت الشرقي)" |
| Sunday, 12:00–13:00, Chicago | "Pam is here Sunday, 12 PM to 1 PM Chicago time" | "…الأحد، من 12 م إلى 1 م (Chicago)" |

**PASS** (`shots/2026-10-18-b74-p3-kept-hours-en-320.png`). The migration puts the hours into
`system_vars.hours` from `pam_hours_config()` as the reply is written. The team's thread draws them
with the same function, `pamSystemText(…, m.systemVars)`. I read that path in the code and didn't
tap it, because the team's screens reach main in b75.
