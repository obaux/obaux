## The note on P4 (for Piper): the "Members interested" circles

- **What happens:** on a program lead's Home, the circles under "Members interested in your
  program" are Tanya ("Saved a new place") and Marcus. They come from the example ranked people
  (`exampleRankedPeople`), but the person page looks people up in `DUMMY_EVERYONE`, which has
  neither. So a tap opens "We could not find that person. Go back to the list and try again."
- **Who sees it:** anyone shown example people, which means Storybook and a demo. Never a real
  account (D-509).
- **I missed this in the run-through.** My script counted "opened a page" as success without
  reading the page. It was there on 17 October in the same five stories.
- **The fix:** either the strip's people come from the same pretend database, or the database gains
  Tanya and Marcus.
