### 1. A program lead's Book a visit: the three kinds listed, and nobody else. **FINDING**

**The three kinds are listed, once each.** Ana (who wrote) shows once, with her message, and
Ben (signed up) and Cora (planned a visit) follow by name. The screen says "Members who wrote to
your program, signed up with it or planned a visit there."

**But someone else is listed too.** Dee wrote to the program, but the database's list
(`my_program_bookable_members`) leaves her out, as it does for anyone blocked either way. She is
still listed, between Ana and Ben (`shots/2026-10-17-rt58-book-list-real.png`).

**Why:**

- `BookForMemberView` lists everyone in the lead's conversations (`useConversations`, from
  `conversation_partners()`), then adds the database's list.
- `conversation_partners()` (0066) doesn't leave out blocked people.
- `my_program_bookable_members` does, and so does `book_visit_for_member`, which refuses with
  `MEMBER_NOT_LINKED` (migration `20261017065103`).

**What she sees:** the lead can pick Dee, choose a day and a time, and tap "Book for Dee". Then:
"We could not save that. This is not your fault. Try again. If it keeps happening, contact Pam."
(`shots/2026-10-17-rt58-book-refused.png`). Trying again can never work. The app has no case for
`MEMBER_NOT_LINKED`; it appears nowhere in `apps/web/src`.

**Expected:**

- the list is the database's list: a person who wrote and can be booked for is in it anyway;
- and, if a refusal still happens, a message that says this person can't be booked, not "try again".

**How sure:**

- high that a person the database refuses can be listed (code and app);
- medium on how often: it needs a block, or a member whose account can't be booked, inside a
  conversation the lead already has.
