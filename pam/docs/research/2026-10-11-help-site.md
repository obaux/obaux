# Help site: baseline usability pass (11 October 2026)

Iris, User Research & Testing Lead. A job from Will through Mira (02:58 UTC): "have the UX
TEAM check usability for glitches and correct scroll jumping per section so everything is
visible and is easy to navigate." This is part 1, the baseline. Part 2 tests the section
menu (scrollspy) once Wren says it is on her branch.

## 1. The flow and the person

**Who:** someone on the public help site who is stuck: a member who can't sign in, a case
manager who wants to send an invite, a program lead. Some are on a small phone, some on a
laptop. Some use only a keyboard or a screen reader.

**Where tested:**

- `pam/apps/site` built from `main` at `769a0fc` (`pnpm --filter @pam/site build`), served
  from `out/`, with every request outside localhost blocked.
- **Not** the live `joinpam.org`: this environment's network policy refuses the host. `main`
  is what deploys there, so the findings should hold. They are unconfirmed on the live site.
- Unmerged branches already in this area were **not** tested:
  - `claude/help-search-everyday-words`
  - `claude/help-post-cant-sign-in`
  - `claude/help-labels-v2`

**How:** Playwright in Chromium.

- **Pages:** `/support/` and all 13 posts.
- **Sizes:** 320×568, 390×844 and 1280×800, light and dark (84 page loads).
- **Measured on each load:** layout shift while loading and while scrolling, sideways
  scroll, clipped text, contrast, tap targets and script errors.
- **Search:** 34 queries, typed the way people type.
- **Keyboard:** tab order and focus, Back after a search, and the screen-reader structure
  (landmarks, headings, live regions).

## 2. What happened

Every post opens and reads end to end at every size and theme. There is:

- no sideways scroll, no clipped text, no contrast failure and no script error;
- no sticky header on `main`, so nothing is hidden behind one.

The problems are in four places: finding the right post, reaching a person, keyboard focus,
and a jump on load.

## 3. Findings

### S — stops people

**S1. Search never offers a way to reach a person.**

- **Page:** `/support/`; every post's "Still need help?"/"Still stuck?" box. All sizes.
- **What I saw:**
  - "Still need help?" says to ask the person who invited you, or a staff member, and
    adds "Inside the app, the Help button is always on screen". Its only button is "Open
    Pam".
  - Someone who can't sign in can't open Pam.
  - Posts say "call Pam" eleven times, but the site has no phone number, `tel:` link or
    email anywhere.
  - Under A28 (Will, 11 October: no phone line; help goes problem → help desk → email
    form), those eleven "call Pam" lines point to something that won't exist.
  - "Always on screen" no longer matches the app. Under A26 (Will, 11 October), Help is on
    Home and one tap from Profile, not on every screen.
- **Who it stops:** a member locked out, with no guide to ask.
- **Shot:** `shots/2026-10-11-help-search-i-cant-login-390.png` (bottom of the page).
- **How sure:** high.

**S2. No visible keyboard focus.**

- **Page:** `/support/` and posts. 1280×800, light and dark.
- **What I saw:** tabbing through the page, these show no ring, outline or change when they
  have focus:
  - Home, Support and Sign in;
  - the search box;
  - every topic and post link;
  - "Open Pam" and the footer links.

  Only the Pam logo and one table region show the browser's ring.
- **Who it stops:** someone using only a keyboard or a switch can't see where they are.
- **Shot:** `shots/2026-10-11-help-no-focus-ring-1280.png`. Focus is on "Texts from Pam" in
  the shot; nothing marks it.
- **How sure:** high (`:focus-visible` matched, computed outline `none`, no shadow).

### W — worth fixing

**W1. Search misses how people type.**

- **Page:** `/support/`, 390×844.
- **What I saw:**

| Typed | Result |
|---|---|
| "i cant login" | 1 result, "Case manager assignments" (wrong) |
| "i can't log in" (with the apostrophe) | 6 results, "Joining Pam" among them |
| "remider", "apointment", "invte", "gide" | no results |
| "password", "spanish" | no results |
| "lost my phone" | "One phone, two sides" (wrong post) |
| "change my number" | "Planning a visit" first (wrong post) |
| "help" | only "Who is my guide?" |

  Several of these may be fixed by `claude/help-search-everyday-words` and
  `claude/help-post-cant-sign-in`, but they aren't merged and I didn't test them.
- **Shot:** `shots/2026-10-11-help-search-i-cant-login-390.png`.

**W2. Back from a result empties the search.**

- **Page:** `/support/`, 1280×800.
- **What I saw:** search "i can't log in", open "Joining Pam", press Back. The box is empty
  and the page is back at the top. The query isn't in the URL either, so it can't be
  shared.

**W3. "One phone, two sides" jumps on load.**

- **Page:** `/support/one-phone-two-sides/`, 1280×800, light and dark.
- **What I saw:** a layout shift of 0.27 about 190ms after load (0.1 is "poor"). The title's
  block goes from 120px to 80px tall and everything below moves up 40px.
- **Smaller jumps:** `joining-as-staff` 0.058 and `planning-a-visit` 0.055, same size.
- **None while scrolling:** shift after load was 0 on every page.

**W4. Some tap targets are under 48px on a phone.**

- **Page:** all pages, 390×844 and 320×568.
- **What I saw:**
  - **Header:** Support and Sign in are 32px tall.
  - **"Open Pam":** 91×32.
  - **Breadcrumb "Support":** 51×20.
  - **Lone links:** 20px tall. For example "Read the privacy policy", "See the full list of
    who…" and "Read the full privacy policy".
  - **Topic lists:** 20px tall per line, with about 12px between them.
  - **Search field:** 23px tall inside a 48px white pill. I didn't check whether tapping the
    pill's edge focuses it.

**W5. Posts have no section anchors yet.**

- **Page:** all 13 posts.
- **What I saw:** no heading has an `id`, and there are no in-page links. A section can't
  be linked to or jumped to. This is the baseline the scrollspy will change; it is not a
  bug today.

**W6. Screen reader: a chatty search, no landmark.**

- **Page:** `/support/`.
- **What I saw:**
  - The results sit in a `aria-live="polite"` region holding the whole list. Each keystroke
    can re-read every title and summary.
  - There is no `role="search"` landmark and no skip link.
  - Esc doesn't clear the box.
- **How sure:** from the page structure only; no real screen reader was used.

**Polish.** At 320 the post title appears three times: the breadcrumb, a grey pill and the
H1. Shot: `shots/2026-10-11-help-what-others-can-see-320-dark.png`.

## 4. What worked (keep it)

- **Tables become cards on a phone.** "Who can see what" reads one card per row at 320.
  Shot: `shots/2026-10-11-help-what-others-can-see-320-dark.png`.
- **Search is honest when it has nothing.** "No results for …" offers the topics and a
  person; "These may be close:" is shown when matches are weak.
- **Nothing clips or scrolls sideways,** and no text fails contrast, in light or dark, at
  320, 390 or 1280.
- **No script errors** on any page.
- **Headings run in order** on posts (H1, H2, H3), and every image has alt text.

## 5. Next

- **Part 2:** the scrollspy, once Wren says it is on her branch. Each "On this page" link
  should land its heading fully visible, below any sticky header. The active section should
  stay right scrolling up and down. The phone menu should be easy to use.
- **A live check**, if the environment is allowed to reach `joinpam.org`.

### Scrollspy (the section menu)

**Phone menu (390 and 320, light and dark):**

- **What works:**
  - The "On this page" bar sticks at the top, 56px tall, and names the current section.
  - The open list fits on screen with 48px rows.
  - Tapping a row closes the list, moves focus to the section, and lands the heading at
    80px, fully below the 57px bar.
- **What doesn't:** Esc doesn't close the open list.

**Do links land the heading visibly?** Yes, every link on every post at 320, 390 and
1280.

**Is the active section right?**

**SP1 (W). "Still stuck?" never becomes active.**

- **What I saw:** at the end of the page, the menu keeps an earlier section highlighted.
  - 1280: all 19 posts.
  - 390: 15 posts.
  - 320: 13 posts.
- Clicking "Still stuck?" at 1280 scrolls to it, but the highlight stays on the section
  before. "Still stuck?" is the A28 route to a person, so it's the one section that
  matters most.
- **Expected:** at the bottom of the page, or after clicking a link, the menu shows that
  section.

**SP2 (W). At 1280, the clicked link isn't the one highlighted.**

- **What I saw:** on most posts, clicking a menu link lands its heading at 24px from the
  top, but the menu marks the section before it. Examples:
  - `planning-a-visit`: "Change a visit" and "Cancel a visit";
  - `start-here`: "The code didn't work".

  It looks like the landing offset at 1280 (24px) is above the line where the menu decides
  which section is active.
- **At 320:** clicking a link always highlights that link.

**SP3 (W). The highlight can point at a section that's off screen.**

- **What I saw:**
  - `one-phone-two-sides`, 1280, at 1920–2110px: "What stays separate" is highlighted
    while only "Still stuck?" is on screen.
  - `cant-sign-in`, 390, at 2172px: "The code didn't work" is highlighted while "Still
    can't get in?" and "Still stuck?" are on screen.
  - `points-and-badges`, 390, at 1352px: "How you earn points today" is highlighted while
    "Badges" and "Who can see your points" are on screen.

**Shot:** `shots/2026-10-11-help-scrollspy-320-dark-and-1280.png`. From left: the phone bar
closed, the list open, and 1280 with "Plan a visit" highlighted while "Change a visit" is
mid-screen.

**How sure:**
- **High** for SP1 and the link landings.
- **Medium** for SP2 and SP3. I judged "active" as the highlighted section's span being on
  screen; Wren's own rule for the line may differ.

## Part 3: Mira's part-2 brief (11 October, 05:20 UTC)

**What I tested:**

- **Help site:** Wren's `claude/compassionate-bohr-mzrchf` at `6d6c093a`, built locally.
  `scripts/usability.mjs` reports "all clear".
- **App:** Dot's `claude/pam-design-contact-pam` at `3281edb`, from its Chromatic Storybook
  build. Stories `member-created--sign-in-wrong-code` and `member-created--get-help`.
- **Focus rule:** `claude/pam-design-focus-text-boxes` (D-523), from its Chromatic build.
- **Sizes and languages:** English and Arabic, at 390 and 320, light and dark.
- **Not tested:** the live app. No accounts were made and nothing was sent.

### The stuck person's path

wrong code → Contact Pam → Help → help desk → search → a post → "Still stuck?"

**CP1 (S). A wrong code has no way to a person.**

- **Screen:** Enter your code, after a wrong code. Story
  `member-created--sign-in-wrong-code`, English and Arabic, 390 and 320, light and dark.
- **What I saw:** the only things on screen are:
  - the message "That code did not work. Try again." (`signin.code.wrong`);
  - Send again;
  - Back;
  - Language;
  - the About / Privacy / Terms footer.

  There's no Contact Pam and no help.
- **Why:** in `SignInScreen.tsx`, Contact Pam is passed only to the `failed` notice. The
  only failure reason is `send`, a code that couldn't be sent. A wrong or expired code never
  reaches it.
- **Who it stops:** someone whose code keeps failing can only try again or start over. The
  rule since A26: help is required on "sign-in and joining, forms and their errors". This is
  the first step of the A28 path.
- **Shot:** `shots/2026-10-11-app-wrong-code-no-contact-ar-320-dark.png`.
- **How sure:** high.

**What works on the rest of the path:**

- **Help** (`member-created--get-help`): Contact Pam is the first row, a 64px row, and goes
  to `joinpam.org/support/`. The same in Arabic at 320 dark (shot:
  `shots/2026-10-11-app-get-help-contact-pam-ar-320-dark.png`). There's no phone row and no
  `tel:` link.
- **The help desk** has search before "Still stuck?", so nobody has to write before seeing
  an answer.
- **"Still stuck?"** is an email link (`hello@joinpam.org`), not a form, so there's nothing
  to send.

**CP2 (W). The help desk is English only.**

- **What I saw:** an Arabic reader taps "تواصل مع Pam" and lands on English pages. The site
  builds a language only once it's signed (`signed-off.json` has only `en`), so this is by
  design until Lena signs. The app's row could say the help desk is in English until then.

### Section menu, by keyboard and motion (help site)

- **What works:**
  - **Phone:** Tab reaches the "On this page" bar after 6 Tabs; Enter opens it, Tab moves
    to a row, and Enter jumps.
  - **After a jump:** focus moves to the section, the heading lands below the bar, and the
    next Tab carries on inside that section.
  - **Motion:** smooth scrolling, unless the reader prefers reduced motion; then the jump
    is instant.
- **SP4 (W). At 1280, the menu comes after the whole post in Tab order.** It takes 14 Tabs,
  after "Still stuck?"'s email link. A keyboard user meets the menu only once they've
  passed everything it would help them skip.
- **SP5 (question).** After a keyboard Enter on a menu link, the section shows a thin 1px
  ring; after a tap, none. D-523 says section jumps show no highlight. Is a keyboard jump
  meant to differ?

### Will's 04:17 rule (D-523), at 390 and 320

**It holds:**

- **Text boxes:** a code box focused by the page shows its own border highlight.
- **Dialogs:** the signature sheet and the More options menu take focus without a ring.
- **Pages with no autofocus:** sign-in, report a place, the conversation and the language
  list don't move focus at all.
- **Tab:** still shows a 3px ring.
