# Help site: baseline usability pass (11 October 2026)

Iris, User Research & Testing Lead. A job from Will through Mira (02:58 UTC): "have the UX
TEAM check usability for glitches and correct scroll jumping per section so everything is
visible and is easy to navigate." This is part 1, the baseline. Part 2 tests the section
menu (scrollspy) once Wren says it is on her branch.

## 1. The flow and the person

**Who:** someone on the public help site who is stuck: a member who can't sign in, a case
manager who wants to send an invite, a program lead. Some are on a small phone, some on a
laptop. Some use only a keyboard or a screen reader.

**Where tested:**

- `pam/apps/site` built from `main` at `769a0fc` (`pnpm --filter @pam/site build`), served
  from `out/`, with every request outside localhost blocked.
- **Not** the live `joinpam.org`: this environment's network policy refuses the host. `main`
  is what deploys there, so the findings should hold. They are unconfirmed on the live site.
- Unmerged branches already in this area were **not** tested:
  - `claude/help-search-everyday-words`
  - `claude/help-post-cant-sign-in`
  - `claude/help-labels-v2`

**How:** Playwright in Chromium.

- **Pages:** `/support/` and all 13 posts.
- **Sizes:** 320×568, 390×844 and 1280×800, light and dark (84 page loads).
- **Measured on each load:** layout shift while loading and while scrolling, sideways
  scroll, clipped text, contrast, tap targets and script errors.
- **Search:** 34 queries, typed the way people type.
- **Keyboard:** tab order and focus, Back after a search, and the screen-reader structure
  (landmarks, headings, live regions).

## 2. What happened

Every post opens and reads end to end at every size and theme. There is:

- no sideways scroll, no clipped text, no contrast failure and no script error;
- no sticky header on `main`, so nothing is hidden behind one.

The problems are in four places: finding the right post, reaching a person, keyboard focus,
and a jump on load.

## 3. Findings

### S — stops people

**S1. Search never offers a way to reach a person.**

- **Page:** `/support/`; every post's "Still need help?"/"Still stuck?" box. All sizes.
- **What I saw:**
  - "Still need help?" says to ask the person who invited you, or a staff member, and
    adds "Inside the app, the Help button is always on screen". Its only button is "Open
    Pam".
  - Someone who can't sign in can't open Pam.
  - Posts say "call Pam" eleven times, but the site has no phone number, `tel:` link or
    email anywhere.
  - Under A28 (Will, 11 October: no phone line; help goes problem → help desk → email
    form), those eleven "call Pam" lines point to something that won't exist.
  - "Always on screen" no longer matches the app. Under A26 (Will, 11 October), Help is on
    Home and one tap from Profile, not on every screen.
- **Who it stops:** a member locked out, with no guide to ask.
- **Shot:** `shots/2026-10-11-help-search-i-cant-login-390.png` (bottom of the page).
- **How sure:** high.

**S2. No visible keyboard focus.**

- **Page:** `/support/` and posts. 1280×800, light and dark.
- **What I saw:** tabbing through the page, these show no ring, outline or change when they
  have focus:
  - Home, Support and Sign in;
  - the search box;
  - every topic and post link;
  - "Open Pam" and the footer links.

  Only the Pam logo and one table region show the browser's ring.
- **Who it stops:** someone using only a keyboard or a switch can't see where they are.
- **Shot:** `shots/2026-10-11-help-no-focus-ring-1280.png`. Focus is on "Texts from Pam" in
  the shot; nothing marks it.
- **How sure:** high (`:focus-visible` matched, computed outline `none`, no shadow).

### W — worth fixing

**W1. Search misses how people type.**

- **Page:** `/support/`, 390×844.
- **What I saw:**

| Typed | Result |
|---|---|
| "i cant login" | 1 result, "Case manager assignments" (wrong) |
| "i can't log in" (with the apostrophe) | 6 results, "Joining Pam" among them |
| "remider", "apointment", "invte", "gide" | no results |
| "password", "spanish" | no results |
| "lost my phone" | "One phone, two sides" (wrong post) |
| "change my number" | "Planning a visit" first (wrong post) |
| "help" | only "Who is my guide?" |

  Several of these may be fixed by `claude/help-search-everyday-words` and
  `claude/help-post-cant-sign-in`, but they aren't merged and I didn't test them.
- **Shot:** `shots/2026-10-11-help-search-i-cant-login-390.png`.

**W2. Back from a result empties the search.**

- **Page:** `/support/`, 1280×800.
- **What I saw:** search "i can't log in", open "Joining Pam", press Back. The box is empty
  and the page is back at the top. The query isn't in the URL either, so it can't be
  shared.

**W3. "One phone, two sides" jumps on load.**

- **Page:** `/support/one-phone-two-sides/`, 1280×800, light and dark.
- **What I saw:** a layout shift of 0.27 about 190ms after load (0.1 is "poor"). The title's
  block goes from 120px to 80px tall and everything below moves up 40px.
- **Smaller jumps:** `joining-as-staff` 0.058 and `planning-a-visit` 0.055, same size.
- **None while scrolling:** shift after load was 0 on every page.

**W4. Some tap targets are under 48px on a phone.**

- **Page:** all pages, 390×844 and 320×568.
- **What I saw:**
  - **Header:** Support and Sign in are 32px tall.
  - **"Open Pam":** 91×32.
  - **Breadcrumb "Support":** 51×20.
  - **Lone links:** 20px tall. For example "Read the privacy policy", "See the full list of
    who…" and "Read the full privacy policy".
  - **Topic lists:** 20px tall per line, with about 12px between them.
  - **Search field:** 23px tall inside a 48px white pill. I didn't check whether tapping the
    pill's edge focuses it.

**W5. Posts have no section anchors yet.**

- **Page:** all 13 posts.
- **What I saw:** no heading has an `id`, and there are no in-page links. A section can't
  be linked to or jumped to. This is the baseline the scrollspy will change; it is not a
  bug today.

**W6. Screen reader: a chatty search, no landmark.**

- **Page:** `/support/`.
- **What I saw:**
  - The results sit in a `aria-live="polite"` region holding the whole list. Each keystroke
    can re-read every title and summary.
  - There is no `role="search"` landmark and no skip link.
  - Esc doesn't clear the box.
- **How sure:** from the page structure only; no real screen reader was used.

**Polish.** At 320 the post title appears three times: the breadcrumb, a grey pill and the
H1. Shot: `shots/2026-10-11-help-what-others-can-see-320-dark.png`.

## 4. What worked (keep it)

- **Tables become cards on a phone.** "Who can see what" reads one card per row at 320.
  Shot: `shots/2026-10-11-help-what-others-can-see-320-dark.png`.
- **Search is honest when it has nothing.** "No results for …" offers the topics and a
  person; "These may be close:" is shown when matches are weak.
- **Nothing clips or scrolls sideways,** and no text fails contrast, in light or dark, at
  320, 390 or 1280.
- **No script errors** on any page.
- **Headings run in order** on posts (H1, H2, H3), and every image has alt text.
