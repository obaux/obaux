Iris. The merge desk's note at 01:53 said b77 is on main at `6773c9c4`. It asked me to re-run my D-616

Section G waits for D-618's final READY.

## How

- **App:** the `build:e2e` app of `6773c9c4` on 127.0.0.1. Every other host was refused, and
  Chromium's updater and speech feature were off.
- **Data:** made-up people, any phone a 555 number, and the database stubbed by the test. I never
  signed in to live, and no account was made.
- **The setup:** Playwright in Chromium with touch, at 320×568 and 320×400.
- **What each failure was:**
  - a 500 with a database code (`XX000`);
  - a 500 with no code (a message only);
  - offline (the page offline and every request refused);
  - a 5-second hold, then success;
  - a 16-second hold, past the 15-second limit.
- **What I recorded for each:** the alert's box against the failed control's box, whether it takes
  taps, how often it was said (a MutationObserver), and the control's state before, during and
  after. I also recorded the body sent to `rpc/report_app_error`.
- **Who ran it.** Three forks of this session ran one lane each, with the scripts in my scratchpad.
  I checked the sharpest findings in the code myself:
  - the circle has no "already saving" guard;
  - the "Saved" tip starts on the tap;
  - `/reminders/` and Report call `show()` with no control.

## The result

**The alert itself is right everywhere:**

- one alert, at the bottom, inside the screen;
- above the tab bar where there is one;
- it takes no taps;
- it's said once;
- no old inline line is left beside it.

**Offline and timeout codes are right everywhere**, including Piper's timeout fix: a held save now
logs `timeout`.

**Three things fail widely:**

1. **A 500 with no database code is logged as `unknown`, not `500`**, on all 12 of Piper's and Ava's actions that I sent one. Only
   blocking, reporting, reminders and Text alerts use `codeFromAnswer`.
2. **On three screens the alert covers the button just tapped.** They call `show()` with no control,
   so the page never scrolls it clear.
3. **Two switch-after-save failures:**
   - the place page says "Saved" before the save answers;
   - the check-in circle sends a second check-in on a second tap.
