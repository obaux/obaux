### Notes on the press layer (for Dot)

1. **Stretched-link cards** (Explore, the case manager's Home and lists, the program lead's
   checklist) need the press too: on `:active` of the card, or `:has(a:active)`, as the clickable
   card has. **This is the gap a member would notice.**
2. **Service choices and the tab bar** show nothing when pressed.
3. **The primary button under reduced motion** shows nothing at all. A shade, or a slightly darker
   fill, would keep "something answered" for people who turn motion off.
