# 2026-10-17 — languages-legal: the language lists show all seven languages (S1)

**Branch:** `claude/lena-language-list-fits` (off main `62ba50e4`, b60 live) · **Lane:** Languages & legal (Lena) · written Saturday 17 October, about 23:30 UTC, after b60. Read `2026-10-17-1745-languages-legal-the-booking-refusals-in-six-languages.md` first.

## What changed

- **S1, Iris's interaction run-through (`claude/research-interaction-run-through`, finding S1: the one finding that stops someone).** Before sign-in, the language list was 300px tall with seven 48px rows. Russian was cut in half and Arabic was below the edge, at 390 and at 320, in the app and in Storybook, on the Sign in globe and on Sign up's "Choose a language". Nothing said there was more, and the person who needs Arabic may read nothing else on the screen.
- **Why:** Astryx caps a `DropdownMenu` (the globe) and a `Selector`'s list (Sign up) at 300px. The globe's menu is capped twice, on the menu and on the popover round it, which decides where it opens. The cap is in Astryx's compiled CSS and has no prop.
- **The fix is the same dropdown, sized to fit, so it ships as a bug fix, not a design change.** `globals.css` has one rule for the three places the cap sits (the menu, the popover round it, the list): `max-block-size: min(400px, calc(100dvh - 32px))`, 400px being seven rows and a bit more. It reaches only a menu or a list whose rows start with the English tag; `TaggedWords` (`@pam/ui`'s `OptionTag.tsx`, the one source every language list uses) now carries `data-pam-tagged`, and that is what the rule looks for. The City list and every other menu keep the 300px cap. On a short phone the viewport still limits the list, which then scrolls instead of running off the screen; a list that does not fit under its box opens above it (Astryx's own flip), which is what Sign up does at both sizes now.
- **`e2e/language-list-fits.spec.ts`:** opens the Sign in globe and Sign up's "Choose a language" at 320×568 and 390×844, in English and in Arabic (the globe moves to the other corner), and checks that every language's row is inside the list's own box, inside the screen, and that the list has nothing to scroll to. It names the rows that fail and where they are. 8 tests × 3 projects.
- **Pictures:** `docs/research/shots/2026-10-17-lena-s1-{before,after}-{signin,signup}-{320,390}.png` (8). Before is main `62ba50e4`'s build, after is this branch's.

## What was wrong, and what missed it

- **The 300px cap was Astryx's, and nobody had opened these two lists at the smallest phone.** Every spec that opens them (`languages.spec.ts`, `join.spec.ts`, `account.spec.ts`) checks the rows' words, tags and direction; none checked that a row can be seen. The fit audit looks at the closed screens and at what a story draws open. The new spec is the first thing that measures the open list.
- **The proof that it fails on main is on the assertion, not on a wait.** The wait is for the list to be open with seven rows on screen and still for two frames, which main satisfies. On main's build, all 8 cases fail on the check itself, for example `ARالعربية: outside its list (815–863 in 523–823), off the screen (815–863 of 844)` and `the list scrolls (344px of rows in 300px)`.
- **Nothing in `apps/web/e2e` measured the old list.** Grep for 300, `max-height`, `scrollHeight`/`clientHeight` and list scrolling: no spec asserts a list's height or that it scrolls; the specs that touch a combobox or an option (`account`, `case-manager-pass`, `directory`, `join`, `languages`, `places`) check words, tags and direction, and still pass.

## Decisions made

- **Same dropdown, not a sheet.** All seven fit at 320×568 on both screens, so nothing here is a design change for Will.
- **Found by the rows, not by the screen.** The rule is keyed to `data-pam-tagged` so a future language list gets it without a change, and the City picker (and `Dropdown.tsx`, which I did not touch) keeps its cap. A list that gets an eighth language is still covered by the spec, which will say if it stops fitting.

## Follow-up, 18 October ~01:30 UTC: it did not hold on top of b62, and what changed

- **What failed.** b63's full run (main `24f3124a` + `95d3fb42`, merge `5ebc4aba`) failed 6 cases, all `language-list-fits.spec.ts` Sign up at 320×568, en and ar, on three projects: "RU… off the screen (560–608 of 568)", "AR… (608–656 of 568)". I merged main (`24f3124a`, `db419de1`; only `STATUS.md` conflicted) and reproduced it.
- **The cause was not where the field is on the page; it is where a click leaves it.** At 320×568 the language field starts at y 489–537, under the sticky Next bar. A click (Playwright's, and a thumb) scrolls the form clear of the bar and leaves the field at about y 260–308 (the document is 823px tall, so the field can never go higher than 234). That is the middle of a 568px screen: about 260px over the field and 260px under it, and seven rows need 344. Astryx tries under the box and then over it (`flip-block`), and when neither side fits it stays under and runs off the screen. Before b62 the field was lower on the page, and the room over it was enough.
- **The fix is a last resort for a list of languages, in `globals.css`** (found by `data-pam-tagged`, as before; `Dropdown.tsx`, `LanguageField.tsx` and `JoinScreen.tsx` untouched): `@position-try --pam-list-centre`, which puts the list over the whole height of the screen, centred on the field, 16px from the top and bottom, tried after under and over. Seven rows then show whatever the field's position: at 320×568 with the field from y 234 to 489, and at 390×844. It covers the field while it is open, as a native picker does, and only when neither side has room. On a screen shorter than the list (320×320) the list keeps 16px each side and scrolls inside (the popover and the box round the list are flex columns that may shrink), with every row reachable. Astryx writes its `position-try-fallbacks` on the element, so the one declaration that replaces them needs `!important`; the rest of the rules do not.
- **What I tried first and left.** Clamping the popover to the room it has (`max-block-size: 100%`) keeps the list on the screen, but the clamped box always "fits", so Astryx never flips to the roomier side: at y 320 the list got 3 rows under the field when 320px were free over it. A floor on the clamp lets it flip, but still leaves five rows and a cut-off Arabic at the exact mid-screen position, which is the person S1 is about. `anchor()` is not valid in a sizing property in Chromium 141, and sizing declared in an `@position-try` option is applied once the option is chosen but is not counted when Chromium decides whether an option fits; that is why the last resort is a position, not a size.
- **The spec** opens Sign up's list with the field where a click leaves it, 400px down (room over it) and 256px down (neither side), at 320×568, and on a 320×320 screen where it must stay on the screen with every row reachable; Sign in, and Sign up at 390×844, as before. 13 tests per project. On the b62 build with the old rule: 5 of 8 on narrow-320 fail (the click, the 256px position, and the short screen, in en and ar), every one on the check, none on a wait.
- **Verified** (merged branch `f755799f` built with `build:e2e` and Storybook, stub env): the new spec 39 of 39 (13 × 3 projects); the whole e2e suite on all three projects **2162 passed, 19 skipped (the specs' own), 0 failed**; fit audit, 7 languages, 320×760 and 320×568, the Dropdown (2), language (17) and sign-in (6) stories: 0 findings; unit tests `@pam/ui` 130, `@pam/web` 188, `@pam/config` 1386; `tsc` clean in web and ui; Storybook's sign-in globe and LanguageField list: 7 of 7 rows, no scroll. ## Follow-up 2, 18 October ~04:00 UTC: Mira's decision, the sheet (B), and what stays of the CSS

- **Why the centred last resort went.** It needed `!important` on `position-try-fallbacks`, because Astryx writes its fallbacks on the popover element (`Layer/useLayer.js:551-559` builds `{positionAnchor, positionArea, positionTryFallbacks}`; `:577-579` spreads it into `style`). `Selector.js:998-1006` and `DropdownMenu.js:726-731` pass `placement` (and `alignment`) but never `positioning: 'custom'`, so Pam cannot reach it, and pam/CLAUDE.md says never `!important`. Mira's call (18 October 01:46): the sheet, Astryx's own `presentation="bottom-sheet"` on the Selector; no exception for Will.
- **What changed.** `LanguageField.tsx` has `presentation="bottom-sheet"` (Sign up only: the globe on Sign in keeps its menu, every other dropdown keeps its popover). `globals.css` loses the position rule, the two flex rules for the popover, and `@position-try --pam-list-centre`: **no `!important` is added by this branch** (the only ones in the file are the reduced-motion reset's, as before). **What stays, and is used:** the one cap lift, `max-block-size: min(400px, calc(100dvh - 32px))` on `.astryx-dropdown-menu:has([data-pam-tagged])` and `[popover]:has(.astryx-dropdown-menu [data-pam-tagged])` (the globe's menu and the popover round it) and on `[role='listbox']:has([data-pam-tagged])` (the list **inside the sheet**: Astryx still caps it at 300px, which I found when the first sheet build failed its own spec: 344px of rows in 300px, Arabic at 560-608 of 568).
- **`bottom-sheet`, not `adaptive`.** `adaptive` is the sheet only at `(max-width: 768px) and (pointer: coarse)` (`hooks/useAdaptivePresentation.js`) and the popover otherwise, so a narrow window with a mouse, and the e2e projects, would test and show the popover and its old problem. One path means what is tested is what is shown.
- **The spec** (`language-list-fits.spec.ts`): Sign in's globe as before; Sign up opens the list with the field where a click leaves it, 400px and 256px down; on a 320×320 screen what holds the list stays on the screen and every row can be scrolled to; and Escape closes the list with focus back on the field, and a pick closes it and switches the page. **On the sheet build: 42 of 42** (14 per project). **On main's real build (`6cc00c76`, old markup): 39 of 42 fail, every one on the spec's own check** (rows outside their list, off the screen, the list scrolls, or a row that cannot be scrolled to); the other three are the Escape-and-pick test, which main passes too (it is behaviour, not S1). None fails on a wait.
- **What the sheet does** (probed on the build, Arabic and English, mouse, keyboard and a touch tap): it is Astryx's `BottomSheet`, a native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()` (`BottomSheet.js:146`), named by the field's label, `aria-modal="true"`, with a level-3 heading and a listbox of options, the chosen one `[selected]`. **Focus** goes to the listbox on open (`SelectorBottomSheet.js:36-37`), and the listbox's active row is **the chosen language** when opened by keyboard or by a tap (the highlight starts on it); with a mouse, the row under the pointer takes the highlight (that is the English highlight over an Arabic tick in the first pictures: the pointer was resting there). **Escape** closes it and focus returns to the field (`BottomSheet.js:97,167,177,193`). **A pick closes it** and focus returns to the field, which now shows the new language. A tap on the dimmed screen and a swipe down close it too (`isSwipeDismissAllowed` for `purpose: 'info'`). **A screen reader** meets the dialog's name, the heading, then seven options, one selected.
- **Not like Pam's other drawers, and not styled round:** the top corners are the same 28px and the grab handle the same 40×5 (both `globals.css` rules are on `.astryx-bottom-sheet`, so they reach it), but the title is Astryx's smaller level-3 heading, and **there is no round button in the corner and no checkmark**: `SelectorBottomSheet` renders `BottomSheet` with only `isOpen`, `onOpenChange`, `finalFocusRef`, `label`, `height: 'hug'` and `purpose: 'info'` (`SelectorBottomSheet.js:57-62`), with no slot for a header control. Will's rule on a97 (a checkmark for a drawer where you pick something) therefore cannot be followed without replacing this sheet with Pam's own (`PamSheet`, on Piper's and Dot's branches, not yet on main) around a list; a pick already closes the sheet, which is what the checkmark would say. Left as Astryx draws it (Mira: no override).
- **The bottom edge.** Astryx's sheet pads for the home bar itself: `BottomSheetPanel.js:99` gives the panel `padding-bottom: calc(env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 0px) + 48px)` (`astryx.css`, `.x1wkw6tp`), and the panel hangs 48px below the screen (`OVERSCROLL_PADDING = 48`, `:40`, `offscreenBlockEndInset`, `:310`; measured: the panel runs 175 to 616 on a 568px screen). On a phone with no inset the last row ends 4px from the edge (516-564 of 568, as in the pictures); with an iPhone's 34px home bar the padding is 82px, 34px of it on screen. I cannot set `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` in this browser, so that is read from the code, not measured.
