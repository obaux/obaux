## What changed
- **The section** `google-maps` (title, p1, p2) is in the seven bundles, between "When we limit an account" and "Changes", and listed in `legal.ts` as `section('terms', 'google-maps', 2)`. The English is the signed draft word for word (`docs/legal/2026-10-31-google-maps-notice-in-the-terms.md`, section 4): "Pam uses Google Maps features and content. Two examples are the map in Trips and the address suggestions when you type a street address to look for places near it." and "Using them is subject to Google's own terms, in the versions in force when you use them: the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service (https://maps.google.com/help/terms_maps/) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy)." p3 ("Our Privacy page says what Google gets when you use them.") is not in the app.
- **Six translations**, formal register as the rest of the Terms (usted, você, 您, вы); "Trips" is each language's own word for the screen (Visitas, Visitas, 预约, 到訪, Визиты, الزيارات, as in `privacy.s.sharing.p5`); "Pam" stays as it is. **The two addresses and Google's two document names are written as in English in every language**, with no translation beside them (my 31 October note had proposed a short one; Mira asked for them as written). "Google Maps" is in Latin letters everywhere, the title included, because that is the name Google's notice asks the app to give. The pronoun "them" is spelled out ("estas funciones y este contenido", "these features and this content") so the sentence stands alone; the articles before Google's titles follow the English noun (es *las … Terms*, *la … Policy*; pt-BR *os … Terms*, *a … Policy*).
- **Arabic and the addresses.** The two addresses sit in plain parentheses inside the right-to-left sentence. Checked in Chromium, in the real Terms page with its own fonts, at 46 widths from 280 to 460px (an address wraps onto a second line in 21 of them): at every width each address reads in order, "(" before it and ")" after it, and the sentence's full stop falls at the line's left end, where an Arabic sentence ends; a wrapped address continues at the right edge of the next line, as in any right-to-left paragraph. Direction marks round the addresses gave the same result, so there are no invisible characters in the text. Chinese uses full-width brackets round the addresses, as the rest of the Chinese Terms uses full-width punctuation.
- **The guard** (`third-party-hosts.test.ts`, from `b394b5d2`) now applies on its own, since the key exists: in every language `terms.s.google-maps.p2` must hold both addresses. New today: `.p1` must name "Google Maps" in every language, the other half of the Maps Platform duty (ToS §3.2.2(a)(i): *notify* users the app includes Google Maps features and content, *and* state that using them is subject to those two documents); without it a later edit could drop the notice and the guard would still pass.

## `legal:date` is not run, and one test is red because of it, on purpose
`legal-updated.test.ts` ("terms: the recorded fingerprint is the one of today's English words, or the date has not moved") compares a fingerprint of the Terms' English with the one recorded the last time `legal:date terms` ran (11 October). The new paragraphs change it, so that single test fails with its own message ("run legal:date terms <the day the change goes live>"), and nothing else does: config suite 73 files, **1,594 passed, 1 failed** (that one). That is the test doing its job: the date is the day the words go live, which is not known until Will says "push b78". I did not skip it, move its record by hand or give the section a provisional date, so no wrong date can ship.
To prove nothing else is waiting behind it, I committed first, then ran `legal:date terms 2026-11-24` and `copy:ack` on a throwaway: the config suite was fully green (73 files, 1,595 passed), and the change was nine files, 15 lines each way (`legal-updated.json`, `terms.updated` in the seven bundles, twelve ledger lines). I then reset the tree to the commit, so the branch is undated.
**For the push day (Mira, in b78's records commit):** `pnpm --filter @pam/config legal:date terms <the day b78 goes live, YYYY-MM-DD, UTC>` then `pnpm --filter @pam/config copy:ack`, then the config suite. A date cannot go back (it must be 11 October 2026 or later). The Privacy date does not move.
