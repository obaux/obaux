## Lessons

- **"Only X can see it" is a claim about the database, not the screens.** The page said nothing about last name and city; the draft that fixed it would have said "only the Pam team", and the grant (0075) and four policies made that false. Check a "who sees" sentence against the grant and the row policies, and name every role in the grant (anon as well as authenticated).
- **A tick label and the screen after it can disagree.** "Text me when Pam opens" promised what `join.waitingDone.yes` admitted could not be done. When checking a promise, read the confirmation screen too.
- **Check retention in three places:** `cron.schedule` in the migrations, `.remove(` in the app, and `on delete` on every foreign key, then say the answer plainly. Here: reports (none, and the reporter's id set null), waiting list (cascade only), staff photos (nothing, and the file outlives the account).
- **A runbook that says "photos" is not a routine that deletes them.** `docs/before-launch.md` lists the delete routine's contents; today's by-hand step is the email alone. A Privacy promise to delete on request is only as true as the step someone actually takes.
- **The guard earns its keep by what it makes you write down:** every gap it found has a named owner (Will, or a card) and an exit (the entry moves from `gap` to `covers`).
