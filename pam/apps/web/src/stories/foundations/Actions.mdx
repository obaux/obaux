# Actions — which one when

| You need | Use | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The screen's one main action | `BigButton` (primary) | One per screen. Put it in the page `footer` so it stays in reach. |
| A second, quieter action | `BigButton variant="secondary"` | Light green. Beside or under the primary, never instead of it. |
| A small way out or alternative | `TextLink` | "Cancel", "Sign", "Send again". Still 48px to hit. |
| Header actions (save, more, help, edit) | Round `IconButton` (ghost, 48px) in the header's `actions` | Icons need an accessible label. |
| Go somewhere | A `MenuList` row | Icon, words, an optional second line, chevron. A row says what it does. |
| Pick one of a few options | `SelectableCard` (grey, with a dial) | The picked card outlines in accent on light green. |
| Pick one of many | Astryx `RadioList` | Long lists, forms. |
| On/off setting | Astryx `Switch` | Text alerts and preferences. |
| Destructive or irreversible | `ConfirmDialog` / Astryx `AlertDialog` | Ask first; say what will be lost and what stays. |
| Something staff do often | `FloatingAction` | A single row pinned above the tab bar on staff Home. |
| Something the member still has to do | Astryx `Banner` (warning) with a `TextLink` | One line: "Sign 4 policies for … before you go · Sign". |

## Touch targets — 48px, and the two exceptions

Everything a person taps is at least **48px** (`--pam-touch-target-min`; the
site-wide floor in `globals.css`). Two things are allowed under it, both on
Will's word, both written down so they stay the only two:

| Exception | Size | Why | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Info tip** | 36px circle (`--pam-touch-target-tip`) | It explains, never acts — missing it costs nothing, and at 48px it outweighs the words beside it (D-368) | `InfoTip`: a small icon that opens a popover (20px padding, a soft shadow, 320px wide or the screen less 32px, text left-aligned). Services hint on Add a program; the signed-every-policy mark |
| **Range tabs** | 38px tall | A compact switch above a calendar (D-355) | The Day / Week / Month `SegmentedControl` on a program lead's Home |

Anything that *does* something — saves, sends, books, navigates — stays at
48px. A new small target needs a decision entry, then a row here.

## Wording

- Verbs that say the outcome: **Plan a trip**, **Book for Jordan**, **Save service**, **Sign**.
- Never "Submit", "OK", "Click here".
- A disabled primary button needs a reason on screen (e.g. "Pick a service").
