# Typography

One family, **Figtree**, self-hosted from `@pam/ui` (`fonts.css`) — nothing loads from Google.
Fallback: the system UI font.

## Pam's sizes (A27)

**The page is 16px**, and so is anything typed into a field. Components say their own size, by what the text is for, and
nothing is under 12px. Will, 11 October: "Leave as is. Make exceptions. Update rules."

| What it is | Size | Weight | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Large title | 34px | 700 | The `h1` of a nested screen (`SubPage`) |
| Page title | 28px | 700 | Tab screens (`--pam-title-size`) |
| Section heading | 20–22px | 700 | "Pick a service", card headings |
| Buttons and rows | 17–18px | 400–600 | `BigButton` (56px high), `MenuList` row labels, card names |
| Reading text | 16px | 400 | Privacy and Terms, policy text, anything a member reads to decide or sign (`--pam-body-text-mobile`) |
| Supporting line | 15px | 400 | A line under a row, the invite line on Sign in, the quiet legal links |
| Component body and labels | **14px** | 400–500 | A field's label and helper line, a list row's meta (distance, hours), a link inside a card, a chip, a compact `Button` |
| Captions and metadata | **12–13px** | 400–600 | Tab-bar labels (12), a card's date and time, a tag or language code, a stat's label, "1 of 3" |
| Under 12px | **never** | | |

**Stays 16px or larger, whatever else a component does:** the screen's action (`BigButton`, 17px) and the field somebody types
into; titles and headings; anything a member reads in order to decide or to sign; and Sign in and joining (title, number field,
button). **Smaller is allowed** for metadata and captions, and for component labels and meta lines, as long as nothing a member
needs in order to act is carried by a small line alone: the next step is a heading, a button or a reading line, never only a
caption. Line height 1.4–1.5 for text of 16px and up. It replaces A23's "nothing a member has to read is smaller than 15px".

Not changed: 48px targets, 56px primary buttons, AAA contrast for body text, scaling to 200%, and the 16px base that the e2e
check reads.

## As built, and noted (A27)

Counted over 120 role screens in Storybook, about 2,300 pieces of text: 355 are 14px, 191 are 13px, 321 are 12px and 7 are 11px
(one caption, "Your badge", over seven stories); 1,400 are 15px or more. The tab bar's labels are 12px (191 of the 321). Three
things are not yet as the rule says, and are with a later job: the 11px "Your badge" caption (should be 12px); on Sign in, the
number field's label and the consent sentence (14px; the rule says Sign in and joining stay larger); and a few links a member acts
on at 14px, which are 48px targets.
