# Writing

How Pam sounds, in one page. The full guide, with examples from Pam's own strings, word
lists and a checklist, is **[Pam's voice](https://github.com/obaux/obaux/blob/main/pam/docs/voice.md)**
(`docs/voice.md`).

## Who we write for

Someone who hasn't used a phone in 8 years, reading on a cracked screen in the sun. If they can
follow it, so can a busy case manager and a program lead.

## Four traits

- **Plain.** Say it the way you'd say it out loud. "That code is too old", not "Invite expired".
- **Warm.** A person, not a system. "Someone at Pam can help you. You do not need to know what to ask for."
- **Steady.** Honest and never alarming. "This is not your fault. Try again. If it keeps happening, contact Pam."
- **Respectful.** People are *members*. Nothing on a screen says why someone needs help.

## The tone moves with the moment

Most personality on the website and in moments of welcome. Calmer in tasks. Plainest in errors and
anything about privacy, terms or what others can see.

## Patterns

- **Screens don't explain (D-608).** No intro paragraph under a title. A row is its name. A feature that isn't on
  yet gets a "Coming soon" badge, never a sentence. What Pam does or never does lives in Privacy or Terms.
- **Say it once (Will, 18 October).** STOP, HELP and rates appear at the first agreement to texts, not again on later
  settings screens. **No legal links on task screens:** Privacy and Terms live in Profile › Legal.
- **Buttons:** a verb, then the thing. "Send me a code", "Contact Pam".
- **Titles:** a task asks the question ("Where is it?"); a result says the outcome ("Your visit is booked").
- **Confirmations say what happens next.** "We check every request by hand. It takes a day or two."
- **Errors:** what happened, not your fault, what to do, how to reach a person (Contact Pam; Pam has no phone line, A28).
- **Empty states:** what will appear here, and the one way to fill it.

## Rules that don't bend

- **The name is "Pam"**, not "PAM". Invite codes keep their `PAM-XXXX` form.
- **Never justice terms** — not in the app, texts, notifications or exports (CI checks this).
- **Seven languages, in step.** Short sentences, no idioms, `{placeholders}` kept whole. How to change
  words: `docs/copy-changes.md`.
- **Texts** start with "Pam:", stay inside their length, carry no emoji, and need a human sign-off.
  A text or email goes out in the person's own language once that language is signed for it, and in
  English until then (A25).
- **Example data** uses "Example …" names and 555 phone numbers. Never a real person's details.
- **Nothing ships unsigned.** Sage signs new sentences; Will signs texts, emails, Privacy and Terms, and new promises (A29).
