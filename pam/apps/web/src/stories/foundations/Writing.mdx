# Writing

- **The name is "Pam"**, not "PAM". Invite codes keep their `PAM-XXXX` form.
- **Plain language, short lines.** Write for someone reading on a cracked phone in the sun.
- **Say what happens next.** "Your visit is booked. We'll text you the day before."
- **Never justice terms.** No "prisoner", "ex-offender", "inmate", "parole violation", or anything
  about a conviction — not in UI, texts, notifications or exports. People are *members*.
- **People, not users.** Members, case managers, program leads, programs.
- **Every string in English and Spanish**, key for key, through i18n.
- **Texts (SMS)** start with "Pam:", stay under 160 characters, carry no emoji, never reveal
  justice involvement, and need a human sign-off before they send.
- **Example data** uses "Example …" program names and 555 phone numbers. Never a real person's
  contact details.
