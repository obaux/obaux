# Principles

These are rules, not preferences. Several are enforced by tests. Generated UI that breaks one is
wrong, however it looks.

## The non-negotiables

1. **48px minimum touch target, everywhere.** Icon buttons, rows, chips, links. A 24px icon sits in
   a 48px hit area. Token: `--pam-touch-target-min`.
2. **One primary action per screen.** One filled `BigButton`. A second action is a
   `BigButton variant="secondary"`, a `TextLink`, or a row — never a second filled button.
3. **Never dead-end.** Every screen has a visible way back (the round back button) and a visible way
   to get help.
4. **18px body text on phones.** Nothing a member has to read is smaller than 15px; supporting
   lines are 15–17px. Token: `--pam-body-text-mobile`.
5. **Astryx only.** No other component library, no hand-rolled controls. Style through StyleX and
   tokens — never inline styles, never a raw hex or px where a token exists.
6. **Never show justice terms.** Not "prisoner", "ex-offender", "inmate", or anything about a
   conviction — anywhere a person can see, including texts and exports.
7. **English and Spanish, key for key.** Every string goes through i18n; nothing is hard-coded.

## How Pam feels

- **Plain over clever.** "Plan a trip", not "Schedule an engagement". One idea per line.
- **Show the next step, not every step.** A screen asks for one thing. Long forms become steps.
- **Big, calm, and certain.** Large type, generous spacing, clear outcomes ("Your visit is booked").
- **The member's thumb decides.** The screen's one action sits at the foot, in reach, while the
  page scrolls under it (see Layout).
- **Motion explains, never decorates.** Under a quarter second, opacity and transform only, and none
  for people who asked their phone for less motion.

## Two screen templates

- **A tab screen** (Explore, Saved, Trips, Messages, Profile) starts with `LargeTitleHeader`.
- **A screen you tap into** starts with `SubPage`: round back button, large title, optional
  subtitle, optional `footer` for the screen's one action.

Do not hand-roll a new header.
